Retro Hoops privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

Retro Hoops collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases.

Your save

Your franchise is stored only on your own device. It never leaves it unless you choose Export, which hands a file to you through the share sheet or a download.

Network

The game does not need the internet to play. The iPhone app does not contact any server. The website version downloads the game files once so it can work offline.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the change is made.
